Privacy Policy

gws CLI (personal OAuth client) · Last updated: 2026-10-08

This OAuth client is a personal command-line tool used solely by the project owner to access their own Google account data. It is not distributed, not offered as a service, and has no third-party users.

Data accessed

  • Gmail (gmail.modify, gmail.settings.basic)
  • Google Drive (drive)
  • Google Calendar (calendar)
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets)
  • Basic profile and email address (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile)

How data is used and stored

  • All data stays on the owner's local machine.
  • Nothing is sent to third parties, external servers, or logged beyond local tool output.
  • OAuth refresh tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's local machine (~/.config/gws-*/credentials.enc, AES-256-GCM with the key held in the OS keychain).

Sharing

No data is shared with anyone. No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party integrations besides Google's own APIs invoked on the owner's behalf.

Retention and deletion

Data is read on demand; nothing is retained beyond command output and local shell history. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions or by deleting ~/.config/gws-*/credentials.enc.
